About us

Beauty is not a women’s club.

We are a one-room lash studio in Woodlands South. Lifts, classics, volume and artsy sets, for anyone who wants their eyes to look more like themselves.

Men included. How you want to look is your decision. Our job is to be good enough at the technique that you get the thing you decided on.

No upsell in the chair, no talking you into a fuller set than you asked for, and no pressure to rebook before you have seen how the first one wears.

  • Gentle, or we do not use it

    Every product is chosen so the lash keeps its strength and the skin around the eye is left alone. If a formula needs to sting to work, it does not come into the studio.

  • The decision stays yours

    We will tell you what your lashes can carry and what we would do. We will not decide for you what you are allowed to want.

  • One artist, start to finish

    The person who maps your lashes is the person who lifts them, and the person who checks them at week six. No handovers.

Questions

Asked at every appointment.

Does it hurt?

No. A lash appointment is one of the more relaxing hours you will spend. Your eyes stay closed, the room is quiet, and most clients drift off. If anything ever stings, pulls, or feels uncomfortable, say so and it gets fixed on the spot.

How long will they last?

The Lift holds five to six weeks on your natural lash cycle. The curl softens as lashes grow through their cycle. It does not drop. Extensions shed with your natural lashes, so they need an infill every two to three weeks to stay full.

If a lift drops early, the guarantee covers it. The next one is on us.

Can I wear mascara with extensions?

You will not need to. That is the point. If you must, use only a water-based formula, never waterproof, and keep all oil-based cleansers and removers away from the lashes. Oil breaks down the bond.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes. No mascara and no lash curler that day. If you can, skip caffeine right before. It makes eyes flutter, and still eyes make for better lashes.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for first-time clients. It happens forty-eight hours before your appointment, takes a few minutes, and it is how we keep sensitive eyes safe. Your booking confirmation includes the patch-test slot.

What if I am running late or need to change the date?

Life happens, but the chair is booked for you. More than fifteen minutes late counts as one session. If you need to change the date, please let us know at least twenty-four hours ahead so the slot can go to someone else.

How do I look after them?

Keep them dry for the first twenty-four hours. No steam rooms, no saunas, no crying at sad movies just yet. After that: no oil-based products near the eyes, do not rub or pick, and brush them daily with the spoolie you take home.
